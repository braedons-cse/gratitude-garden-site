This permanently erases your Gratitude Garden account and everything in it. You can also do it in the app: Me → Delete account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name and password
  • Every journal entry, with its mood and photo
  • Your garden, coins, purchases, streaks and level

It happens at once and can't be undone. Copies in our providers' backups and logs are overwritten shortly after, and crash reports (which aren't linked to your account) expire within 90 days. See the privacy policy.
